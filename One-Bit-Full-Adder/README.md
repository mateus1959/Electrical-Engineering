**1-Bit Full Adder PCB**

A two-layer printed circuit board designed in Altium Designer that implements a 1-bit binary full adder using SN74LS-series XOR, AND, and OR logic ICs.

The circuit accepts three binary inputs (A, B, and Carry-In) and produces two outputs (Sum and Carry-Out). It operates at 5 V and uses through-hole components.
